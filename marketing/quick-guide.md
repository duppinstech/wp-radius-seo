# Radius SEO quick guide

## What it is

A WordPress plugin that publishes local landing pages and service-area hubs from one template plus a place library.

## The four screens that matter first

1. **Settings → License.** Paste the signed key and click Save.
2. **Settings → Service areas.** Add a center and a radius in miles.
3. **Location library.** Import places. Include latitude and longitude.
4. **Templates, then Deploy.** Write the blueprint with tokens, then deploy it.

## Tokens

- `{{city}}` comes from the place.
- `{{company-name}}` and `{{phone-number}}` come from site replacers.
- `{fast|quick}` stores one of those words on that page.

## After publish

Open a landing and a service-area hub on the front of the site. The place name should appear in the title. Deploy the same template again after an edit. You should still have one page for that place, with the new text.

## When something fails

Open **Radius → Logs**. Then **Radius → Support** and describe what you were trying to do and what the screen said. That email goes to support@duppinstech.com. Call (240) 397-8221.
