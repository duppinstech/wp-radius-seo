# Deactivate

On Plugins, Deactivate under Radius SEO opens a dialog. Bulk deactivate and network deactivate do not open the dialog and do not delete Radius data.

## Choices

- Keep all data and deactivate turns the plugin off and leaves landing pages, service areas, templates, places, and settings stored.
- Delete all Radius data lists what will be removed. Confirm delete removes it, then deactivates. Back returns to the first choice.
- Cancel, Close, Escape, or a click outside the dialog leaves the plugin active and deletes nothing.

After Confirm delete, keep the Plugins window open until Radius SEO turns off. If the delete stops, the plugin stays active. Choose Delete all Radius data again, or Keep all data and deactivate. Pages and places already removed stay removed.

Deleting the plugin files after it is off removes the plugin and the update-check cache. It does not remove pages or settings that were kept.
